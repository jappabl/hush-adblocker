# Hush privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-10-23*

Hush is a browser extension that blocks ads, trackers, malware sites and unwanted redirects.

**What Hush collects:** nothing. Hush has no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no servers of its own. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information or browsing data.

**What stays on your device:** your per-site settings (sites where you turned off ad or redirect blocking), the downloaded filter lists, and a count of blocked items for the current tab. These are kept in your browser's local extension storage and never leave your device.

**Network requests Hush makes:** every 12 hours Hush downloads public filter lists from their publishers (easylist.to, ublockorigin.github.io, pgl.yoyo.org, cdn.jsdelivr.net for HaGeZi, malware-filter.gitlab.io). These are plain downloads of public files; no information about you or your browsing is sent with them beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address, seen by those publishers' servers).

**Why Hush needs access to all websites:** to block ads and trackers on every page, hide ad slots, and stop click-hijacking scripts, Hush must run on the pages you visit. It inspects pages only inside your browser to do that, and does not record or transmit what you visit.
