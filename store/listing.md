## Detailed description
Hush blocks ads and trackers like other blockers do, and also fixes the part they miss: pages that hijack your clicks.

Click anywhere on some sites and a new tab opens, or the page jumps to a scam. Click a real button and nothing happens because the first click was swallowed by an invisible ad layer. Hush stops that:

• Clicking a blank part of a page never opens a tab or sends you elsewhere.
• Real buttons and links work on the first click, even when an ad script tries to steal it.
• Invisible ad layers, fake play buttons and pages wrapped in an ad link are switched off before you click.
• Pages can't trap the Back button or redirect you to scams after a few seconds.
• When something is blocked, a small notice appears with a way through if you did want it.

Ad and tracker blocking:
• EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock filters, Peter Lowe's list, HaGeZi Pro and malware lists (uBlock Badware, URLhaus).
• Lists update themselves every 12 hours.
• Ad slots are hidden so pages don't show empty gaps.
• YouTube video ads are removed.

Per-site switches in the toolbar button let you turn off ad blocking or redirect blocking for any site.

Privacy: Hush has no account, no analytics and no servers. Everything runs in your browser; the only network requests it makes are downloading the public filter lists.
